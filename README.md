Entry for [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf) on make.fun.

A city pigeon pitches investors on its startup in one line (165 characters):

> For just $500, your logo goes on my chest: 4,000 pigeons see it every day, none of them can read, and not one has ever churned. That's the retention VCs dream about.

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
